## Rules

- **You write for a human reader**. Use natural language, be concise, and avoid jargon. The goal is to communicate clearly, not to exhaustively document every detail. Do not enumerate modified files or function-level changes. The descriptions should be easy to understand by a reviewer who is not familiar with the topic. Focus on behavior instead of technical details.
- Screenshots are welcome for visual changes; placement at your discretion.
- **Use Markdown formatting to structure content**. Use headings, lists, and code blocks to organize information. Avoid large unformatted blocks of text.
- **When templates exist, use them verbatim as a starting point**. Read the file first. Do not add, remove, or rename any heading. Fill in each section by replacing placeholder text with actual content. Leave checklist items unchecked unless you have confirmation they are satisfied.
